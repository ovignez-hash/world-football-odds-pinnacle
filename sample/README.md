## Rules applied to every cell

- **Times** are ISO 8601 UTC with the T and the Z (`2026-09-20T15:30:00Z`), plus a Unix epoch column.
  Excel and LibreOffice keep this form as text: it is never converted into a local date.
- **No cell that a spreadsheet can rewrite.** Scores, minutes and shoot-outs are separate integer columns
  (never "1-0", "90+2" or "73:02"). Formations are digits: `4231` means 4-2-3-1, `442` means 4-4-2
  ("4-4-2" in a cell becomes 04/04/2002 in French Excel). Handicap lines are decimals (`-0.25`, never "0.0,-0.5").
  Seasons are `2016/17` or `2019`. Leading "-", "+", "=" or "@" are removed from names.
- Every cell of every file was audited against these Excel conversion patterns at write time: **0 risky cells**.
- **Empty cell = absent from the source.** Nothing is imputed, interpolated or averaged across bookmakers.
- A suspended price ("-") is an empty cell with `Suspended = 1`: kept, not deleted.

## Pre-match, in-play and closing

- A quote is **Pre-match** when it carries no score AND was recorded before the kickoff anchor K. Everything
  else is **In-play**.
- **K** is the scheduled kickoff. When Bet365's live clock (tick time minus its minute, minutes 1 to 15) shows
  that the match started before the scheduled time, K is that start minus 2 minutes. Without a Bet365 live clock,
  K is also bounded by the first quote that carries a score, minus 2 minutes. K is never later than the scheduled
  kickoff, so a delayed kickoff closes the market at the scheduled time.
- **Opening** = first valid pre-match quote, **Closing** = last valid pre-match quote, per bookmaker and market,
  each with its own time. A closing price is never an in-play price.
- The source records a quote when the price changes. A `_Close_Time` hours before kickoff therefore means the
  price did not move after that time, or that the bookmaker stopped quoting the market: the data cannot tell the
  two apart. Compare the `_Close_Time` columns before comparing closing prices across books.

#### Said plainly - known limitations

- **Pinnacle**: closing prices regular from 2018/19 on FT_1X2, FT_Asian_Handicap, FT_Total_Goals. Its Asian handicap is regular from 2023/24. Lower coverage in 2023/24 (see the season table).
- **Betfair (Sportsbook)**: closing prices regular from 2023/24 on FT_1X2.
- **Betsson**: closing prices regular from 2022/23 on FT_1X2, FT_Total_Goals.
- **Unibet**: closing prices regular from 2017/18 on FT_1X2, FT_Total_Goals.
- **Bet365 markets**: FT 1X2, Asian handicap and total goals regular from 2016/17; HT_1X2 regular from 2018/19, FT_Total_Corners regular from 2019/20, HT_Asian_Handicap regular from 2020/21.
- **Bet365 corner markets** often carry a single pre-match quote: opening = closing on 49 % (FT) and 40 % (HT) of the matches priced.
- **Pinnacle in-play quotes** are numerous only in 2026/27 (227,673); earlier seasons hold at most 17,087 per season. Pinnacle's live clock is mostly not a whole minute ('3 - 46', '1H47''), so `Minute` stays empty on those rows.
- **Implausible source prices are kept as given**: 6 opening/closing 1X2 quotes with an overround outside -0.5 % to 25 %, 5 extreme handicap or total lines, and one Bet365 opening with home and away apparently swapped (Osasuna - Mallorca, 2022-05-22).
- **Closing times differ by bookmaker** (median gap between the last recorded FT 1X2 pre-match quote and kickoff: Pinnacle 3 min, Betfair 6 min, Betsson 8 min, Bet365 24 min, Unibet 53 min).
- **Kickoff times**: for 4 matches the source's scheduled kickoff is more than 3 hours later than the real start read from Bet365's live clock (for example Sporting Gijon - Leganes (2026-03-02), Ternana - Brescia (2017-10-03), Santa Clara - Benfica (2021-01-04)). `Kickoff_UTC` keeps the source's value; their pre-match markets are cut at the real start.
- **Data starts on 2016-08-28** (Championship 2016-09-09, 2. Bundesliga 2016-09-09, Ligue 2 2016-09-09): the first matchdays of 2016/17 are missing. **2026/27** stops at 2026-09-30.
- **Lineups**: formations are present from 2019/20. Lowest lineup coverage: Ligue 2 (75.7 %).
- **Minute-by-minute statistics** start in 2017/18 and cover most matches from 2020/21.
- **Events** are the source's text timeline: it keeps some events it later cancelled (goals disallowed by VAR,
  rescinded cards; a repeated ordinal marks them). Goals in events match the 90-minute score on
  98.434 % of matches; the match tables carry the official score.
- **Team statistics**: goals equal the 90-minute score on 99.98 % of rows.
- Odds are UTC and come from one data provider. No cross-provider reconciliation is claimed.

#### Play-offs, extra time, penalties and neutral venues

- **Extra time** (76 matches) and **penalty shoot-outs** (59) are recorded only in a deciding
  Play-Offs match (single match or second leg). Extra_Time = 1 needs proof in the source: a goal scored in extra time,
  an event dated from the 106th minute, or the source's extra-time score line. A shoot-out without that proof keeps
  Extra_Time = 0 (23 of the 59 shoot-outs): extra time may have been played without a goal
  or an event the source dates beyond the 105th minute.
- **Two-legged ties** (255): `Leg` 1 and 2 when two teams meet twice in Play-Offs with home and away
  reversed, without another play-off match in between. 5 ties have only their second leg here (the source files the first leg with the lower division): `Leg` 2, with the source's aggregate score.
- **Neutral_Venue** compares the stadium's country with the home team's country code from the source: 1 = neutral
  (0 matches), 0 = home ground country (88,458), empty = unknown (4,157).
  The source gives the same country code (gb) to Scottish, Welsh and Northern Irish clubs: The New Saints at home in
  Oswestry (England) count as home; so does FC Vaduz (Swiss league, Swiss code) at home in Liechtenstein.

- **Macedonian First League (North Macedonia)**: Unibet was not collected for this league.

The odds, the match sheet, the events and the statistics are there; see `Lineups_Pct` in the Coverage file, season by season.
- **Lineups are partial** (under half of the matches) for 9 more leagues: Belarus (7 %), Estonia (11 %), Faroe Islands (8 %), Hungary (9 %), Ireland (10 %), Iceland (9 %), Lithuania (12 %), Scotland (39 %), Serbia (28 %).
- **Venue unknown** for 4,157 matches (4.5 %); `Neutral_Venue` is empty on 4,157 matches.
- **Round** is filled on 87,659 matches (94.6 %): it stays empty where the source's matchday numbers are not reliable (for example the Finnish league, whose numbering is broken in several seasons) and in Play-Offs.
- **Kickoff times**: for 14 matches the source's scheduled kickoff is more than 3 hours later than the real start read from Bet365's live clock, for example: Ararat Armenia - Shirak, played 2025-11-30 (source kickoff 2025-12-01 10:00 UTC); Botev Plovdiv - Lokomotiv 1929 Sofia, played 2026-07-20 (source kickoff 2026-07-21 18:30 UTC); Panathinaikos - Xanthi, played 2018-05-06 (source kickoff 2018-05-07 16:00 UTC). `Kickoff_UTC` keeps the source's value; the pre-match markets are cut at the real start.
- **Score field of the source**: on 36 of the 59 penalty shoot-outs the source's own final score adds the shoot-out to the goals. This product never uses it: goals after 90 minutes, after extra time and in the shoot-out are separate columns.
- **Coverage changes over time** (table *Coverage over time*). First match covered: Unibet 2017-04-15, minute-by-minute statistics 2017-06-12, Pinnacle 2018-09-27, Betsson 2022-07-03, Betfair (Sportsbook) 2023-11-03. - **Data starts on 2016-08-28**: the first season of each league is partial (its first matchdays are missing). The last seasons stop at 2026-09-30.

  rescinded cards; a repeated ordinal marks them). Goals in events match the 90-minute score on

- **Team statistics**: goals equal the 90-minute score on 99.97 % of rows
  of matches without extra time.

#### Knockout stages, extra time, penalties and neutral venues

- **Knockout stages** (911 matches): Play-In, Round of 16, Quarter-finals, Semi-finals, Third Place, Final
  and Play-Offs. Single matches (262) have an empty `Leg`.
- **Two-legged ties** (288): `Leg` 1 and 2 when two teams meet twice in a knockout stage with home and
  away reversed, at most 21 days apart, without another knockout match between them (or as written in the lexicon:
  see *Legs* in Data integrity); the second leg decides. 1 tie was decided by a third match (`Leg` 3). 7 ties have only their second leg (`Leg` 2) and 5 only their first leg (`Leg` 1): the other leg is missing from the source; `Home_Aggregate` / `Away_Aggregate` are filled only when the source gives them.
- **MLS best-of-three series** (24 series, Round of 16 from 2023): `Leg` is the game number; every game is decided on the day (a draw after 90 minutes goes to penalties, without extra time).
- **Extra time** (43 matches) and **penalty shoot-outs** (116) are recorded only in a deciding
  knockout match (single match, second leg, third match, game of a best-of-three series). Extra_Time = 1 needs proof
  in the source: a goal scored in extra time, an event dated from the 106th minute, or the source's extra-time score
  line. A shoot-out without that proof keeps Extra_Time = 0 (90 of the 116 shoot-outs): several
  of these leagues go straight to penalties after 90 minutes, and extra time may also have been played without a goal
  or an event the source dates beyond the 105th minute.
- **Neutral_Venue** compares the stadium's country with the home team's country code from the source: 1 = neutral

#### Said plainly - known limitations

- **Betfair and Betsson** were not collected for Argentina 2016/17 to 2019/20 (1,422 matches) and USA 2020 MLS is Back (51 matches): `Betfair_Pct` and `Betsson_Pct` are empty in the Coverage file for a season with none of its matches collected.
- **Lineups** are absent or almost absent from the source for 2 leagues: Bolivia, Venezuela. The odds, the match sheet, the events and the statistics are there; see `Lineups_Pct` in the Coverage file, season by season.
- **Matches missing from the source** (stages or matches never collected): Bolivia 2024: play-off Royal Pari - Real Oruro: first leg only (2024-12-21); the second leg is missing from the source; Colombia 2017 Apertura: knockout phase incomplete in the source (3 quarter-final first legs and the final's second leg); Colombia 2017 Finalizacion: second legs only for the quarter-finals; their first legs are missing from the source; Colombia 2018 Apertura: knockout phase (quarter-finals, semi-finals, final) missing from the source; Colombia 2018 Finalizacion: knockout phase (quarter-finals, semi-finals, final) missing from the source; Ecuador 2018: final between the stage winners missing from the source; Mexico 2018/19 Apertura: second legs only for the semi-finals; their first legs (2018-12-06) are missing from the source; Venezuela 2016: season final Zamora - Zulia (winners of the Apertura and the Clausura): first leg only (2016-12-04); the second leg in Maracaibo is missing from the source; Venezuela 2017 Clausura: 8 regular-season matches missing from the source; Venezuela 2021: final of 2021-12-11 missing from the source.
- **Venue unknown** for 1,343 matches (4.3 %); `Neutral_Venue` is empty on 1,343 matches.
- **Round** is filled on 25,394 matches (81.9 %): it stays empty where the source's matchday numbers are not reliable for that season and stage, and in knockout stages.
- **Kickoff times**: for 7 matches the source's scheduled kickoff is more than 3 hours later than the real start read from Bet365's live clock, for example: CA Tigre - CA Aldosivi, played 2025-09-20 (source kickoff 2025-09-21 15:00 UTC); Platense - Defensa y Justicia, played 2025-03-03 (source kickoff 2025-03-04 23:00 UTC); Banfield - CA Independiente, played 2025-03-03 (source kickoff 2025-03-04 22:30 UTC). `Kickoff_UTC` keeps the source's value; the pre-match markets are cut at the real start.
- **Score and clock on in-play quotes** are the bookmaker's own: Pinnacle almost never sends a numeric clock (`Minute` empty) and Betfair often sends a partial score (`Score_Home` / `Score_Away` empty). Nothing is filled in from another bookmaker.
- **Minute-by-minute statistics carry no recording time before 2021-05-11**: `Timestamp_UTC` and `Epoch` are empty on the 5,535 matches played before (2,102,874 rows, 25.2 %); `Minute` and `Value` are always there.
- **Minute clock on the night the clocks go back in Europe**: in 25 matches played that night (last weekend of October, 2019 to 2025), the source's minute clock ran one hour behind for part of the match. On those stretches `Minute` is 60 below the real match minute, which shows as 2,139 rows with a negative `Minute` (ARG 247, BRA 610, CHI 32, COL 86, ECU 96, MEX 597, USA 423, VEN 48). The rows are kept as the source recorded them. `Timestamp_UTC` and `Epoch` are not shifted (given from 2021-05-11): they place each row in the match.
- **Score field of the source**: on 59 of the 116 penalty shoot-outs the source's own final score adds the shoot-out to the goals. This product never uses it: goals after 90 minutes, after extra time and in the shoot-out are separate columns.
- **Coverage changes over time** (table *Coverage over time*). First match covered: Unibet 2017-04-15, minute-by-minute statistics 2017-06-10, Pinnacle 2018-09-27, Betsson 2022-07-03, Betfair (Sportsbook) 2023-11-03. Months with a gap: Pinnacle 2023-09 to 2023-11 and 2026-07 to 2026-08; Unibet 2026-07 to 2026-08; Betfair (Sportsbook) 2024-11 to 2025-08 and 2026-07 to 2026-08; Betsson 2025-12 and 2026-07 to 2026-08; minute-by-minute statistics 2018-02 to 2019-09 and 2026-07.

#### Knockout stages, extra time, penalties and neutral venues

- **Knockout stages** (94 matches): Quarter-finals, Semi-finals, Third Place, Final and Play-Offs. Single matches (16)
  have an empty `Leg`.
- **Two-legged ties** (39): `Leg` 1 and 2 when two teams meet twice in a knockout stage with home and
  away reversed, at most 21 days apart, without another knockout match between them; the second leg
  decides.
- **Extra time** (9 matches) and **penalty shoot-outs** (5) are recorded in a deciding
  knockout match (single match or second leg). Extra_Time = 1 needs proof in the source: a goal scored in extra time,
  an event dated from the 106th minute, or the source's extra-time score line. A knockout shoot-out without that proof
  keeps Extra_Time = 0 (1 of the 5): extra time may have been played without a goal
  or an event the source dates beyond the 105th minute. The source's extra-time key on league matches is ignored.
- **Group-stage shoot-outs**: in the 2026 J1 100 Year Vision League group stage a draw after 90 minutes went straight
  to penalties (2 points to the winner, 1 to the loser): 51 shoot-outs, `Extra_Time` = 0.
- **Neutral_Venue** compares the stadium's country with the home team's country code from the source: 1 = neutral
  (none in these leagues), 0 = home ground country (16,312), empty = unknown (1,017).
  A neutral ground inside the home team's own country (China's 2020 and 2021 hub venues) counts as 0: the rule compares countries, not grounds.

- **Kickoff times**: for 4 matches the source's scheduled kickoff is more than 3 hours later than the real start read from Bet365's live clock, for example: Qingdao West Coast - Wuhan Three Towns, played 2025-03-02 (source kickoff 2025-03-03 06:00 UTC); FAR Rabat - JS Soualem, played 2025-04-23 (source kickoff 2025-04-24 05:45 UTC); Bizertin - Olympique de Beja, played 2026-09-05 (source kickoff 2026-09-05 18:42 UTC). `Kickoff_UTC` keeps the source's value; the pre-match markets are cut at the real start.

- **Score and clock on in-play quotes** are the bookmaker's own: Pinnacle almost never sends a numeric clock (`Minute` empty) and Betfair often sends a partial score (`Score_Home` / `Score_Away` empty). Nothing is filled in from another bookmaker.
